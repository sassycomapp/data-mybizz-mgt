Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to the information you give when you sign up on mybizz.live. Last updated: 22 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Mybizz is run by David Coleman, trading as Mybizz. For any question about your information, email hello@mybizz.live.

What is collected

  • Your email address, when you sign up.
  • Your consent: the date and time you ticked the consent box and confirmed your email address.

Why it is collected

To email you when Mybizz launches, and to send occasional Mybizz news and offers. This happens only with your consent, which you give by ticking the box and confirming your email address.

Where it is kept

Your email address is kept on the Mybizz mailing list at EmailOctopus, the email service that sends Mybizz emails, under EmailOctopus's privacy policy. It is never sold or shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept

Until you unsubscribe. Every Mybizz email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from the list.

Bot protection and cookies

The sign-up form uses Google reCAPTCHA to keep out automated sign-ups. reCAPTCHA collects information about your device and use of the page and sets cookies, under the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. The Mybizz page itself sets no cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information held about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email hello@mybizz.live. If you are unhappy with the answer, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
