## 2. Naming Rule

| Name | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Mybizz** | The brand: every brand channel (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok), the logo and the products Mybizz CS and Mybizz FDA |
| **Mybizz-Live** | The company: the LinkedIn company page, the UK registration, Paddle, invoices and legal policies |
| **mybizzlive** | Every handle and username, matching the web address mybizz.live |

- **Why two names:** "Mybizz" is the name people see and remember. Many other businesses use similar names, so every handle and address carries "live", which keeps Mybizz findable and distinct.
- **Personal profiles carry David's real name.** LinkedIn and Facebook require a real person behind every company page, and both suspend personal accounts named after a business.

## 5. B2 — LinkedIn

**David's personal profile** (signs in with `sassycomapp@gmail.com`) carries the minimum: name, photo, the YouTube banner, headline beginning "Founder, Mybizz", location Knysna, Western Cape, South Africa, website `mybizz.live` (type Company), one Experience entry (Founder, Mybizz-Live, self-employed, remote, March 2025 to present), and the About text. 2. Name **Mybizz-Live**; public URL `mybizzlive`; website `https://mybizz.live`; industry **Software Development**; size **0–1 employees**; type **Privately held**; logo: the profile picture; tagline: the short bio. Tick the confirmation box, then **Create page**.
3. David, as Super admin, on the new page: **Edit page → Header**: the LinkedIn banner. **Edit page → Overview**: the long description. **Save**.
4. On David's profile, edit the Experience entry and pick **Mybizz-Live** from LinkedIn's list, so the page logo shows beside the role.

**Check:** linkedin.com/company/mybizzlive shows the disc, the banner and the description.

## 8. B5 — TikTok

TikTok allows one account per phone number, and David's number belongs to his existing TikTok account. That account is the Mybizz account.

1. **☰ → Settings and privacy → Account → Account information → Email:** `mybizzlive@gmail.com`, confirmed with the emailed code.
2. **☰ → Settings and privacy → Account → Password:** a password, so the account signs in with `mybizzlive@gmail.com` and that password, never through a Google sign-in.
3. **Edit profile (pencil, top left):** name **Mybizz**; username `mybizzlive`; the profile picture; bio: the short bio. Pronouns and the AI self stay empty. TikTok offers this account no website field.
4. The account type stays as it is: TikTok offers this account no free switch to a business account, and posting works without one. TikTok's paid verified business account is not used.

**Rules:** TikTok allows one username change every 30 days. After several failed code attempts, TikTok blocks codes for that email address for at least a day: enter each code once, and stop at the first refusal.

**Check:** tiktok.com/@mybizzlive shows the disc, the name Mybizz, the short bio and no old videos.
