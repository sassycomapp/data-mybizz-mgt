## 2. Naming Rule

| Name | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Mybizz** | The brand: every brand channel (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok), the logo and the products Mybizz CS and Mybizz FDA |
| **Mybizz-Live** | The company: the LinkedIn company page, the UK registration, Paddle, invoices and legal policies |
| **mybizzlive** | Every handle and username, matching the web address mybizz.live |

- **Why two names:** "Mybizz" is the name people see and remember. Many other businesses use similar names, so every handle and address carries "live", which keeps Mybizz findable and distinct.
- **Personal profiles carry David's real name.** LinkedIn and Facebook require a real person behind every company page, and both suspend personal accounts named after a business.

## 5. B2 — LinkedIn

**David's personal profile** (signs in with `sassycomapp@gmail.com`) carries the minimum: name, photo, the YouTube banner, headline "Founder, Mybizz", location Knysna, Western Cape, South Africa, website `mybizz.live` (type Company), one Experience entry (Founder, Mybizz-Live, self-employed, remote, March 2025 to present), and the About text. Skills, interests and instant messaging stay empty.

**Page creation needs:** more than one connection (the profile connects with LinkedIn Open Networkers, found by searching "open networker" → People), and an account at least one day old. LinkedIn may also ask for workplace verification, which is not offered in South Africa; LinkedIn support handles that case through a support request from the Help Center.

**The company page:**

1. Open `https://www.linkedin.com/company/setup/new/` → **Company**.
2. Name **Mybizz-Live**; public URL `mybizzlive`; website `https://mybizz.live`; industry **Software Development**; size **0–1 employees**; type **Privately held**; logo: the profile picture; tagline: the short bio. Tick the confirmation box, then **Create page**.
3. On the new page: **Edit page → Header**: the LinkedIn banner. **Edit page → Overview**: the long description. **Save**.
4. On David's profile, edit the Experience entry and pick **Mybizz-Live** from LinkedIn's list, so the page logo shows beside the role.

**Check:** linkedin.com/company/mybizzlive shows the disc, the banner and the description.

## 8. B5 — TikTok Business Account

TikTok allows one account per phone number, and David's number belongs to his existing TikTok account (signed in with `hikwagga@gmail.com`). That account becomes the Mybizz account.

1. In the TikTok app, sign in to that account.
2. **Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Account → Account information → Email**: change to `hello@mybizz.live` and confirm with the emailed code. Set a password.
3. **Account information → Username**: `mybizzlive`. **Edit profile**: name **Mybizz**, profile picture, the short bio, and the website when TikTok offers the field.
4. Delete every old video.
5. **Settings and privacy → Account → Switch to Business Account**; category **Software & apps**.

**Retry rule:** after several failed code attempts, TikTok blocks all codes for about a day. Stop and retry the next day, not sooner.

**Check:** tiktok.com/@mybizzlive shows the disc, the short bio and no old videos.
