## 2. Five Build-in-Public Posts

### Post 1: The origin story
> I used to run life-support operations in the Congo jungle and Antarctica.
>
> In remote operations there is one rule: keep systems simple and make sure no message is ever missed.
>
> Then I looked at how small consulting firms handle new enquiries. A contact form that lands in a busy inbox. A mobile number answered between meetings. A "let me check my diary" reply the next day.
>
> By then, the customer has booked someone else.
>
> So I built Mybizz FDA: a front desk that replies in minutes, in your voice, offers real times from your calendar and follows up, with your approval on every reply.
>
> Building it in public, from £0. Follow along.

### Post 2: The five-minute problem
Run this test yourself first and replace the bracketed numbers with your own results before posting.

> Last week I tried to enquire, as a customer, at [20] independent consulting firms in the UK.
>
> - [14] had a contact form that disappeared into an inbox
> - [4] listed a mobile number with no reply until the next day
> - [2] let me book a call straight away
>
> When someone enquires at 7:30 pm, they usually contact two or three firms. The first helpful reply with a real time wins.
>
> That's what Mybizz FDA does: it reads the enquiry, drafts a reply from your own services and FAQs, offers your real free times, and waits for your one-click approval.

### Post 3: Why approve-before-send matters
> Most consultants won't let AI talk to their clients, for good reason: they're afraid it will invent a price, promise a deadline or sound like a robot.
>
> I wouldn't let an unchecked bot email my clients either.
>
> So Mybizz FDA starts every account in approve-before-send:
> 1. An enquiry arrives by email, website or text.
> 2. Mybizz FDA drafts a reply in your voice using only your own services, FAQs and calendar.
> 3. You approve it with one click, and the record shows who approved it.
>
> Technology should protect your reputation, not gamble with it.

### Post 4: Bootstrapping, honestly
> No investors. No loans. £0 ad budget.
>
> My daily routine to win the first Mybizz FDA clients:
> - research 5 independent consultants or small firms
> - record a 60-second video showing where their enquiry flow loses people
> - send 5 honest, personal messages
> - improve the product from every reply
>
> You don't need a big budget to prove a useful product. You need real conversations with people who feel the problem every day.

### Post 5: Let customers talk to your website
> More and more customers would rather speak than type.
>
> Mybizz FDA's website chat has a microphone. Your customer asks a question out loud; the assistant answers from your own services and FAQs, out loud, and offers a real time to talk.
>
> It always says it's an AI assistant. It never imitates anyone's voice. And it never promises what you haven't approved.
>
> Try it: mybizz.live.
