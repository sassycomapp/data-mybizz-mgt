# Script — FDA-4 Your Data, Your Storage

## Intro (Sarah on camera, Google Flow, 8 s)

Your customer list is your most valuable asset. So where is it kept, and who holds the key?

## Narration

**Scene 1 — The list behind the business**
*On screen: a simple customer list, names and conversations, glowing as the business's core.*

Behind every consulting or service business there's a list. The people who've enquired. The clients you've worked with. The conversations, the questions, the meetings booked, the follow-ups sent.

That list is years of effort. It's your reputation written down. And for most small businesses, it's the most valuable thing they own.

So it's worth asking a simple question about any software you use: where does that list actually live, and who controls it?

**Scene 2 — Rented, not owned**
*On screen: the list inside a locked box labelled "someone else's servers".*

With most online tools, your data lives on the provider's servers, in the provider's format, under the provider's rules. You can see it while you pay. But it isn't really in your hands.

That's fine until something changes. The price goes up. A feature you rely on disappears. The service no longer suits you. And then you discover how hard it is to leave, because leaving means getting your data out, if you can, in a form you can use.

That's what being locked in feels like. You stay, not because the tool is right, but because moving is painful.

**Scene 3 — A different arrangement**
*On screen: the list moving into a box labelled with the business's own name.*

Mybizz FDA, the Mybizz Front Desk Assistant, is built on a different arrangement. Your data is stored in your own storage account, not Mybizz's.

When you sign up, you open your own free storage account with Cloudflare, a large and well-established internet company. You switch on its storage service and give Mybizz limited access. Mybizz then creates a private storage area for your business, with a key that reaches that area and nothing else.

The account is in your name. It stays in your name.

**Scene 4 — Backed up every night**
*On screen: a moon, then a small copy of the list moving into the business's own storage.*

Every night, a backup of your data is saved to your own storage. Your customers, your leads, your conversations, your bookings.

The detailed history of what happened in your front desk, such as who approved which reply and when, stays in the app for thirty days so you can check it easily. After that, it moves to your own storage too, so nothing is lost and nothing piles up.

**Scene 5 — Your business, separate from everyone else's**
*On screen: several separate houses, one per business, instead of one shared building.*

Your front desk runs as its own separate instance, with its own database. Your customers are not mixed in with other businesses' customers in one big shared system.

It's a simple principle: one business, one front desk, one set of data.

**Scene 6 — Export whenever you like**
*On screen: a download arrow; a complete file appears.*

You can export all of your data at any time. Not just when you're leaving, and not by asking for permission. Whenever you want a copy, it's yours to take.

**Scene 7 — Leave when you want**
*On screen: a door opening; the business walks out carrying its list.*

And if one day Mybizz FDA is no longer right for you, you can leave. There's no contract. You pay month to month.

When you go, your storage account stays with you, because it was always yours. Your nightly backups are in it. Your history is in it. Nobody needs to hand anything back, because it never left your hands.

Even if a payment is missed, your data is never held back from you.

**Scene 8 — What Mybizz keeps**
*On screen: a very short list: contact details, subscription status, billing references.*

So what does Mybizz itself keep? Very little. Basic contact details for your account, your subscription status, and a minimal billing record. The payment details themselves are held by Paddle, the company that handles Mybizz's billing.

Your customers' details are not Mybizz's to keep.

**Scene 9 — Privacy in the AI as well**
*On screen: an AI icon with a shield.*

The same thinking applies to the AI. Your front desk uses AI through your own AI account, so you can see what's used and set a monthly spending limit. And the text of what the AI reads and writes isn't stored in logs. Only the basic facts needed to keep the service running are kept.

**Scene 10 — Why this matters more with AI**
*On screen: an AI icon next to the customer list; a question mark over the list.*

This question matters even more now that AI is part of everyday software. When a service reads your customers' messages and helps reply to them, it's fair to ask what happens to those messages afterwards.

Your customers trusted you with their details, not a software company. Keeping their information in your own storage, under your own name, is one of the simplest ways to honour that trust.

**Scene 11 — Safe even if things change**
*On screen: a calendar flipping forward years; the business's storage stays steady.*

Software companies change. Prices change, products change, and sometimes companies change hands.

Because your backups live in your own storage account, your records don't depend on any of that. Whatever happens to any service you use, the copy of your business's history stays where you put it: with you.

**Scene 12 — Leaving, in practice**
*On screen: three simple steps: export, keep your storage, cancel.*

So what does leaving actually look like? You export your data, if you want an extra copy. You keep your storage account, with your backups already in it. And you stop your monthly subscription.

That's it. No exit fees, no waiting for files, no awkward conversation. The arrangement is designed so that leaving is easy, which is exactly why you can relax about staying.

**Scene 13 — Simple to set up**
*On screen: a short sign-up checklist: open the free storage account, switch it on, invite Mybizz.*

Setting this up is part of signing up, and it only takes a few minutes. You open your free storage account, switch on the storage service, and invite Mybizz in with limited access. The setup steps guide you through it.

From then on, the backups happen on their own. You don't have to remember anything.

**Scene 14 — Stay because it's right**
*On screen: the business with its list, calm, beside the Mybizz logo.*

The best reason to stay with any service is that it keeps earning its place, not that leaving is too hard.

With Mybizz FDA, your customers, your history and your backups live in your own storage, under your own name, from the first day to the last.

Your customers. Your data.
