# Script — CS-1 Six Tools, One Platform

**Task:** 41 in `marketing-plan.xlsx` (Video Plan sheet): Script EN
**Video:** CS-1, long-form marketing, target 8 minutes, landscape
**Presenter:** Sarah (on-camera intro, Gemini voice narration)
**Aimed at:** owners running their business across separate tools
**Purpose:** shows that one platform replaces the five or six separate tools a business runs
**App screens:** none

---

## Intro (Sarah on camera, Google Flow, 8 s)

A website, a booking app, a CRM, email marketing, invoicing. Five bills, and none of them talk.

## Narration

**Scene 1 — The way most businesses are run online**
*On screen: six tool icons around a small business: website, booking, payments, CRM, email marketing, invoicing.*

Ask a small consulting or service business how it runs online, and you'll usually hear a list.

There's a website, on one service. A booking system, on another. Payments, through a third. A CRM, a place to keep customer details, if they've got that far. Something for email newsletters. Something else for invoices. Often an AI writing tool on top.

Five, six, sometimes seven separate tools. Each one chosen for a good reason. Each one with its own login, its own monthly bill and its own way of doing things.

**Scene 2 — None of them were made to work together**
*On screen: arrows trying to connect the tools, most of them broken.*

The trouble is that these tools were never designed to work together.

A booking in one doesn't create a contact in another. A payment in one doesn't create an invoice in another. A new customer on your website doesn't appear in your email list unless someone puts them there.

That someone is usually you. The owner becomes the human link between the tools, copying details from one screen to the next.

**Scene 3 — The owner becomes the IT department**
*On screen: the owner surrounded by settings screens and help articles.*

Then there's the learning. Every tool has its own settings, its own updates, its own support pages. When something stops connecting, you're the one who has to work out why.

Without meaning to, the owner of a small business becomes a part-time IT administrator. Hours go into keeping the tools running, hours that could have gone into clients.

**Scene 4 — No clear picture of the business**
*On screen: customer details scattered across four separate boxes.*

And because the information lives in several places, there's no single, reliable view of the business.

Who are your best customers? Which services bring in the most? Which invoices are still unpaid? Which enquiries never turned into bookings? Each answer sits in a different tool, if it's recorded at all.

**Scene 5 — One platform instead**
*On screen: the six tools fold into one shape with the Mybizz logo.*

Mybizz CS takes a different approach. It isn't a booking app, a CRM or a website builder. It's all of them at once, in one connected platform.

Your business gets its own professional website, with a blog and landing pages. Booking and scheduling for the way you deliver your services. Payments through your own Stripe and PayPal accounts. A CRM, a sales pipeline and quotes. Email on your own domain, with a shared team inbox and email marketing. Invoicing and accounts. Time tracking. Reviews. A customer portal. AI help with writing. And voice access to your business from your phone.

**Scene 6 — Everything connected from the start**
*On screen: a booking flows into a contact, a payment, an invoice and the accounts, in one smooth line.*

Because it's one platform, everything is connected from the start.

When a customer books, they become a contact automatically. When they pay, the payment is recorded against them. When the work is done, the invoice is created, and it flows into your accounts. When they open your newsletter, it's the same customer record.

Nothing to copy. Nothing to connect. Nothing to keep in sync.

**Scene 7 — Set up once**
*On screen: a short checklist being ticked: your details, your services, your team, your look.*

You set up your business once: your details, your services, your team, your colours. Then the website, the booking, the emails and the invoices all use the same information.

There's no stack of integrations to build, and no technical degree required.

**Scene 8 — A team that grows without the bill growing**
*On screen: team members joining one by one; the price stays the same.*

Each team member gets their own sign-in, access level and email address. Each person who delivers a service gets their own calendar. Each salesperson works their own deals.

And the price doesn't change as your team grows. Mybizz CS is one price for the whole business, whether you're on your own or a team of ten.

**Scene 9 — Your own, private instance**
*On screen: one separate house per business, each with its own key.*

Each business gets its own private instance of Mybizz CS, on its own domain, with its own database. Your data isn't mixed with anyone else's, and it's backed up to your own storage every day.

**Scene 10 — Email on your own domain**
*On screen: team email addresses with the business's own domain, and a shared inbox.*

Email is part of it too. Each team member gets their own email address on your business's own domain, not a free webmail address. Shared addresses, such as an enquiries desk, land in a team inbox where everyone can see what's been answered.

The same customer records sit behind your email, your bookings and your invoices, so you always know who you're talking to.

**Scene 11 — A day in a joined-up business**
*On screen: a customer's journey drawn as one line: website, booking, payment, session, invoice, review.*

Follow one customer through a day. They find your website and read a blog post. They book a session, choosing a time from your real availability. They pay a deposit through your own payment account. The reminder goes out automatically. After the session, the invoice for the balance is created, the payment is recorded, and a little later they're invited to leave a review.

One customer, one record, and not one detail copied by hand.

**Scene 12 — One view of your business**
*On screen: a simple dashboard: bookings, income, outstanding invoices, top services.*

Because it all lives in one place, you can see your business clearly. Bookings this week. Income this month. Invoices still unpaid. Your best services and your best customers.

No more piecing the picture together from four different tools.

**Scene 13 — Built for consulting and service businesses**
*On screen: examples: a consultant, a coach, a therapist, a designer, a trainer.*

Mybizz CS is built for consulting and service businesses of every kind: consultants and coaches, therapists and trainers, designers and agencies, businesses that see clients in person and businesses that work entirely online.

Whether you sell your time by the hour, run sessions and packages, or deliver fixed-price pieces of work, the platform is built around how you actually serve your customers.

**Scene 14 — One platform. One price.**
*On screen: the single platform beside the brand statement.*

Five or six tools, each doing one job, leave you to do the joining. One platform does the whole job, and lets you get back to running your business.

One platform. One price. Your customers. Your data.
