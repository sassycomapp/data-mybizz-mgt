# Script — CS-3 A Business, Not a Listing

**Task:** 48 in `marketing-plan.xlsx` (Video Plan sheet): Script EN
**Video:** CS-3, long-form marketing, target 8 minutes, landscape
**Presenter:** Sarah (on-camera intro in her Google Flow voice; narration in her narration voice)
**Aimed at:** owners listed on marketplaces among hundreds of others
**Purpose:** shows the business with its own website, brand and booking, competing on its own terms
**App screens:** none

---

## Intro (Sarah on camera, Google Flow, 8 s)

On a marketplace, you're one listing among hundreds. Customers compare you on price, then scroll on.

## Narration

**Scene 1 — One of hundreds**
*On screen: a long grid of near-identical listings; one of them is the business.*

Search for a service in almost any town, and a marketplace will show you a long list. Photos, star ratings, prices. One business after another, all in the same format.

Somewhere in that list is you. Same template, same layout, same small square of space as everyone else.

**Scene 2 — What a listing can't say**
*On screen: the business's listing; most of its story is cut off.*

A listing is built to make businesses easy to compare. That's useful for the customer. It's not so useful for you.

There's very little room to explain what makes you different. Your approach. Your experience. The way you work with people. The details that would make the right customer choose you are squeezed into a few lines, if there's room for them at all.

So customers compare what's easy to compare. Usually, that's price.

**Scene 3 — Competing on the platform's terms**
*On screen: the list re-ordering itself; the business moves down.*

On a marketplace, you compete on the platform's terms. The platform decides where you appear, which businesses are promoted, and what you're allowed to show.

Your neighbours in the list are your competitors, and the platform shows you side by side, at the very moment the customer is deciding.

**Scene 4 — You are a business**
*On screen: the business steps out of the grid and stands on its own.*

But you're not a listing. You're a business, with your own name, your own way of working and your own reasons for doing what you do.

Mybizz CS gives you the tools to look and work like one.

**Scene 5 — Your own website**
*On screen: a clean, professional website with the business's own name, colours and services.*

With Mybizz CS, your business has its own professional website, on its own domain. Choose a homepage and page templates, add your colours, and describe your services the way you want to.

There's a blog to share what you know, and landing pages for your campaigns. Customers who arrive see only you. No competitors alongside. No list to scroll.

**Scene 6 — Your own booking**
*On screen: the website's "Book now" button opening the business's own booking calendar.*

Customers book directly on your website, in your booking flow, under your brand.

Mybizz CS handles booking for the way you actually work: appointments in person or online, group sessions, packages, or fixed-price pieces of work ordered and delivered online. Customers can pay at booking, pay a deposit, pay on the day or receive an invoice, through your own Stripe and PayPal accounts.

And there's no commission on your bookings or your payments.

**Scene 7 — Your own reputation**
*On screen: customer reviews appearing on the business's own website, approved by the owner.*

The reviews your customers leave belong on your own website, where they build your reputation, not the platform's. You approve each review before it appears, and you can add the testimonials clients have sent you directly.

**Scene 8 — Your own customer relationships**
*On screen: a customer record with bookings, payments and messages in one place.*

Every customer who books becomes your customer, in your own database. You can email them from your own domain, send them your newsletter, and welcome them back to your customer portal.

The relationship is yours to build.

**Scene 9 — Found on your own terms**
*On screen: the business's website appearing in search results and shared on social media.*

You decide how people find you: through your website, your blog, your social media, your referrals, your own email list. Each one builds your name, not someone else's platform.

**Scene 10 — Telling your story**
*On screen: an About page and a blog post on the business's own website.*

On your own website, you finally have room to explain yourself. An About page that tells people who you are and why you do this work. Service pages that describe exactly what a customer gets. Blog posts that show what you know and answer the questions customers really ask.

The right customers read that and recognise that you're the person they were looking for. That's something a listing can't do.

**Scene 11 — Every way you deliver**
*On screen: four icons: in person, online, group session, delivered work.*

Your services don't have to fit someone else's template. Mybizz CS is built for the way consulting and service businesses really work: sessions at your premises or at the customer's, meetings online, group classes and workshops, packages of sessions, and pieces of work that are ordered, paid for and delivered without an appointment at all.

**Scene 12 — Your colours, your domain**
*On screen: the business's website, emails and invoices all showing the same colours and logo.*

Everything carries your identity. Your website uses your colours and your logo. Your emails come from your own domain. Your invoices carry your name. Customers see one consistent business at every step, from the first visit to the final invoice.

**Scene 13 — A portal for your customers**
*On screen: a customer signing in to see bookings, invoices and documents.*

Your customers can also sign in to their own portal on your website, to see their bookings, their invoices and their history with you. It's the kind of polish customers notice, and it makes your business feel established.

**Scene 14 — Professional from day one**
*On screen: a new business launching with a complete website, booking and email in one go.*

For a new business, this matters even more. Instead of starting as one more listing, you can start as a complete business from the first day: a website, booking, payments, email on your own domain and invoicing, all set up together.

First impressions count, and you only get one.

**Scene 15 — Compete on your own terms**
*On screen: the business's own website standing alone, beside the brand statement.*

On a marketplace, you're one of hundreds. On Mybizz CS, you're a business, with your own website, your own brand, your own booking and your own customers.

You compete on your own terms.

One platform. One price. Your customers. Your data.
