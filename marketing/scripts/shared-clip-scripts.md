# Scripts — Shared Clips

**Tasks:** 3, 7, 8, 9 and 10 in `marketing-plan.xlsx` (Video Plan sheet): scripts of SC-1 to SC-4 in English, French, German, Spanish and Italian
**Used by:** the outro clips (SC-1, SC-2) and the teardown voice clips (SC-3, SC-4)
**Source:** marketing plan sections 6.4 and 8.2

---

## 1. Rules

- Each shared clip is one fixed line, the same in every video that uses it (marketing plan section 6.4).
- Speech runs at about 2.5 words a second, so an 8-second clip carries at most 18 words and a 6-second clip at most 13.
- The outro clips show `mybizz.live` on screen.
- The teardown intro names Sarah as an AI presenter, because a teardown goes to one person and no voice may pass as a real person (marketing plan section 13). The prospect's company is named in the teardown's own narration, which opens with "This is a short video from Mybizz, made for [Company Name]." (marketing plan section 8.2).
