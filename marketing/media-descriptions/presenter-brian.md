# Media Description — Brian, Presenter

**Task:** 2 in `marketing-plan.xlsx` (Video Plan sheet)
**Used by:** task 6 (Brian base image), task 13 (Brian's Google Flow voice) and every Google Flow clip of Brian
**Source:** marketing plan section 6.3

---

## 1. Who Brian is

Brian is the Mybizz technical presenter for explainer videos, and the voice of the help videos. He is an AI presenter, and every video says so.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Age | 45 |
| Look | Calm, clear and capable; a patient expert who makes the product easy, not a salesperson |
| Dress | Smart-casual business: a charcoal grey jacket over a plain light blue open-collar shirt, no tie, no pattern, no logo, no text |
| Hair | Short, neatly cut, dark brown with a touch of grey at the temples |
| Face | Clean-shaven |
| Eyes | Grey-blue |
| Accessories | None in frame; no glasses, no watch |
| Expression | Relaxed, a light closed-mouth smile, looking straight into the camera |
| Voice | Mild UK accent (chosen in tasks 13 and 16, not in the image) |

## 2. Base image rules

The base image is the first frame of every Google Flow clip of Brian, so it is made to work for every video shape.

- **Landscape 16:9**, the shape of the explainer videos.
- **Framed head and upper chest, centred,** with space around him, so the same clip crops cleanly to portrait 9:16 (Shorts) and square 1:1 (Instagram and Facebook feeds).
- **Mouth closed, hands out of frame, face and mouth unobstructed,** so Google Flow animates speech cleanly.
- **Plain, softly blurred background** of a bright modern office in neutral tones, the same setting as Sarah's, with no readable text, screens, logos or brand names.
- **Soft, even front light;** natural skin; photographic, not illustrated.
- **One person only.**

## 3. Prompt for the base image (Google Flow)

Paste as written:

> Photorealistic portrait photograph of a 45-year-old man, calm, clear and capable, looking straight into the camera with a light closed-mouth smile. Short, neatly cut dark brown hair with a touch of grey at the temples. Clean-shaven. Grey-blue eyes. He wears a charcoal grey jacket over a plain light blue open-collar shirt, no tie, no glasses, no jewellery. Framed from the top of the head to the upper chest, centred, with generous space on both sides. Background: a bright, modern, minimal office in neutral tones, softly out of focus, with no text, screens or logos. Soft, even front lighting, natural skin texture, sharp focus on the face. Landscape 16:9. One person only, hands not visible.

## 4. Description for every clip of Brian

Every Google Flow clip of Brian starts from the base image ("Frames to Video") and carries this line, word for word, before the clip's own action:

> Brian, a clean-shaven 45-year-old man with short dark brown hair greying at the temples and grey-blue eyes, in a charcoal grey jacket over a light blue open-collar shirt, speaking to camera in a bright modern office.

## 5. Choosing the base image

Generate several images from the prompt in section 3 and choose one that passes every check:

1. He looks 45, calm and credible as a patient expert.
2. Face, eyes, teeth line and hands show no AI faults (extra fingers, uneven eyes, warped collar).
3. Hair, jacket, shirt and background match section 1, with no text or logos anywhere.
4. His head and shoulders still fit when the image is cropped to 9:16 and to 1:1.
5. He does not resemble a known real person.

The chosen image is saved as `marketing/brand-assets/presenters/brian-base-image.jpg`, and the base image is never regenerated once clips are made from it.
