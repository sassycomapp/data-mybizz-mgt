# Media Description — Brian, Presenter

**Task:** 2 in `marketing-plan.xlsx` (Video Plan sheet)
**Used by:** task 6 (Brian base image), task 13 (Brian's Google Flow voice) and every Google Flow clip of Brian
**Base image:** `marketing/brand-assets/presenters/brian-base-image.jpg`

## 1. Who Brian is

Brian is the Mybizz technical presenter for explainer videos, and the voice of the help videos. He is an AI presenter, and every video says so.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Age | 45 |
| Look | Calm, clear and capable; a patient expert who makes the product easy, not a salesperson |
| Dress | Smart-casual business: a charcoal grey jacket over a plain light blue open-collar shirt, no tie, no pattern, no logo, no text |
| Hair | Short, neatly cut, dark brown with a touch of grey at the temples |
| Face | Clean-shaven |
| Eyes | Grey-blue |
| Accessories | None in frame; no glasses, no watch |
| Expression | Relaxed, a light closed-mouth smile, looking straight into the camera |
| Setting | A bright, modern office, softly out of focus: a window with plants on one side, wooden bookshelves on the other; no readable text or logos |
| Voice | Crisp UK English; the Google Flow custom voice in section 6 |

## 2. Base image rules

The base image is the first frame of every Google Flow clip of Brian, so it is made to work for every video shape.

- **Landscape 16:9**, the shape of the explainer videos.
- **Framed head and upper chest, centred,** with space around him, so the same clip crops cleanly to portrait 9:16 (Shorts) and square 1:1 (Instagram and Facebook feeds).
- **Mouth closed, hands out of frame, face and mouth unobstructed,** so Google Flow animates speech cleanly.
- **Softly blurred office background,** the same kind of setting as Sarah's, with no readable text, screens, logos or brand names.
- **Soft, even front light;** natural skin; photographic, not illustrated.
- **One person only.**

## 3. How the base image was made (Google Flow)

The base image was made in Google Flow (Image, Landscape 16:9) with this prompt:

> Photorealistic portrait photograph of a 45-year-old man, calm, clear and capable, looking straight into the camera with a light closed-mouth smile. Short, neatly cut dark brown hair with a touch of grey at the temples. Clean-shaven. Grey-blue eyes. He wears a charcoal grey jacket over a plain light blue open-collar shirt, no tie, no glasses, no jewellery. Framed from the top of the head to the upper chest, centred, with generous space on both sides. Background: a bright, modern office in neutral tones, softly out of focus, with a window and plants on one side and wooden bookshelves on the other, no text, screens or logos. Soft, even front lighting, natural skin texture, sharp focus on the face. Landscape 16:9. One person only, hands not visible.

## 4. Description for every clip of Brian

Every Google Flow clip of Brian starts from the base image ("Frames to Video") and carries this line, word for word, before the clip's own action:

> Brian, a clean-shaven 45-year-old man with short dark brown hair greying at the temples and grey-blue eyes, in a charcoal grey jacket over a light blue open-collar shirt, speaking to camera in a bright modern office.

## 5. The base image

`marketing/brand-assets/presenters/brian-base-image.jpg` (2752 × 1536 pixels) is Brian's base image. It passes every check:

1. He looks about 45, calm and credible as a patient expert.
2. Face, eyes, collar and smile show no AI faults, and no hands are in frame.
3. Hair, jacket, shirt and background match section 1, with no text or logos.
4. His head and shoulders fit when the image is cropped to 9:16 and to 1:1.
5. He is an AI-generated person, not a real person.

The base image is never regenerated once clips are made from it.
