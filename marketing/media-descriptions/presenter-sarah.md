# Media Description — Sarah, Presenter

**Task:** 1 in `marketing-plan.xlsx` (Video Plan sheet)
**Used by:** task 5 (Sarah base image), task 12 (Sarah's Google Flow voice) and every Google Flow clip of Sarah
**Source:** marketing plan section 6.3

---

## 1. Who Sarah is

Sarah is the Mybizz presenter for marketing videos (long form and Shorts) and teardowns. She is an AI presenter, and every video says so.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Age | 40 |
| Look | Warm, confident, approachable; a business owner's peer, not a celebrity or a salesperson |
| Dress | Smart-casual business: a feminine, delicately tailored navy jacket over a plain white top, no pattern, no logo, no text |
| Hair | Shoulder-length, medium blonde, softly styled, kept behind the shoulders and clear of the face and mouth |
| Eyes | Hazel |
| Accessories | Small plain stud earrings only; no glasses, no necklace, no watch in frame |
| Expression | Relaxed, a light closed-mouth smile, looking straight into the camera |
| Voice | Mild UK accent (chosen in tasks 12 and 15, not in the image) |

## 2. Base image rules

The base image is the first frame of every Google Flow clip of Sarah, so it is made to work for every video shape.

- **Landscape 16:9**, the shape of the long-form videos.
- **Framed head and upper chest, centred,** with space around her, so the same clip crops cleanly to portrait 9:16 (Shorts) and square 1:1 (Instagram and Facebook feeds).
- **Mouth closed, hands out of frame, face and mouth unobstructed,** so Google Flow animates speech cleanly.
- **Plain, softly blurred background** of a bright modern office in neutral tones, with no readable text, screens, logos or brand names.
- **Soft, even front light;** natural skin; photographic, not illustrated.
- **One person only.**

## 3. Prompt for the base image (Google Flow)

Paste as written:

> Photorealistic portrait photograph of a 40-year-old woman, warm, confident and approachable, looking straight into the camera with a light closed-mouth smile. Shoulder-length medium blonde hair, softly styled and kept behind her shoulders, clear of her face. Hazel eyes. She wears a feminine, delicately tailored navy jacket over a plain white top, small plain stud earrings, no glasses, no other jewellery. Framed from the top of the head to the upper chest, centred, with generous space on both sides. Background: a bright, modern, minimal office in neutral tones, softly out of focus, with no text, screens or logos. Soft, even front lighting, natural skin texture, sharp focus on the face. Landscape 16:9. One person only, hands not visible.

## 4. Description for every clip of Sarah

Every Google Flow clip of Sarah starts from the base image ("Frames to Video") and carries this line, word for word, before the clip's own action:

> Sarah, a 40-year-old woman with shoulder-length medium blonde hair and hazel eyes, in a feminine, delicately tailored navy jacket over a plain white top, speaking to camera in a bright modern office.

## 5. Choosing the base image

Generate several images from the prompt in section 3 and choose one that passes every check:

1. She looks 40, warm and credible as a business owner's peer.
2. Face, eyes, teeth line and hands show no AI faults (extra fingers, uneven eyes, warped earrings).
3. Hair, jacket and background match section 1, with no text or logos anywhere.
4. Her head and shoulders still fit when the image is cropped to 9:16 and to 1:1.
5. She does not resemble a known real person.

The chosen image is kept with the presenter clips (marketing plan section 6.4) as `sarah-base-image.png`, and the base image is never regenerated once clips are made from it.
