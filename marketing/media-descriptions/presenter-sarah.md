# Media Description — Sarah, Presenter

**Task:** 1 in `marketing-plan.xlsx` (Video Plan sheet)
**Used by:** task 5 (Sarah base image), task 12 (Sarah's Google Flow voice) and every Google Flow clip of Sarah
**Base image:** `marketing/brand-assets/presenters/sarah-base-image.jpg`
**Source:** marketing plan section 6.3

---

## 1. Who Sarah is

Sarah is the Mybizz presenter for marketing videos (long form and Shorts) and teardowns. She is an AI presenter, and every video says so.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Age | About 40 |
| Look | Warm, friendly, confident and approachable; a business owner's peer, not a celebrity or a salesperson |
| Dress | Smart-casual business: a feminine, delicately tailored soft camel jacket over a pink broderie (eyelet) blouse with a small ruffled collar; no logo, no text |
| Hair | Medium blonde with lighter highlights, in soft waves falling just past the shoulders, clear of the face and mouth |
| Eyes | Hazel |
| Accessories | None in frame; no glasses |
| Expression | Relaxed, a warm closed-mouth smile, looking straight into the camera |
| Setting | A bright, modern office, softly out of focus: a window with greenery and plants on one side, wooden bookshelves on the other; no readable text or logos |
| Voice | Crisp UK English; the Google Flow custom voice in section 6 |

## 2. Base image rules

The base image is the first frame of every Google Flow clip of Sarah, so it is made to work for every video shape.

- **Landscape 16:9**, the shape of the long-form videos.
- **Framed head and upper chest, centred,** with space around her, so the same clip crops cleanly to portrait 9:16 (Shorts) and square 1:1 (Instagram and Facebook feeds).
- **Mouth closed, hands out of frame, face and mouth unobstructed,** so Google Flow animates speech cleanly.
- **Softly blurred office background** with no readable text, screens, logos or brand names.
- **Soft, even light;** natural skin; photographic, not illustrated.
- **One person only.**

## 3. How the base image was made (Google Flow)

The base image was made in Google Flow (Image, Landscape 16:9) from a square AI-generated reference image of Sarah, added to the prompt box, with this prompt:

> Keep this exact woman: the same face, hair, makeup, soft camel jacket and pink broderie top. Recreate the photo as a photorealistic landscape 16:9 image, framed from the top of her head to the upper chest, centred, with generous space on both sides. Background: a bright, modern, minimal office in neutral tones, softly out of focus, with no text, screens or logos. Change only her expression: a warm, friendly, light closed-mouth smile, looking straight into the camera. Soft, even front lighting, natural skin texture, sharp focus on the face. One person only, hands not visible.

## 4. Description for every clip of Sarah

Every Google Flow clip of Sarah starts from the base image ("Frames to Video") and carries this line, word for word, before the clip's own action:

> Sarah, a woman of about 40 with wavy medium blonde hair and hazel eyes, in a soft camel jacket over a pink broderie blouse, speaking warmly to camera in a bright modern office.

## 5. The base image

`marketing/brand-assets/presenters/sarah-base-image.jpg` (2576 × 1438 pixels) is Sarah's base image. It passes every check:

1. She looks about 40, warm and credible as a business owner's peer.
2. Face, eyes and smile show no AI faults, and no hands are in frame.
3. Hair, jacket, blouse and background match section 1, with no text or logos.
4. Her head and shoulders fit when the image is cropped to 9:16 and to 1:1.
5. She is an AI-generated person, not a real person.

The base image is never regenerated once clips are made from it.
