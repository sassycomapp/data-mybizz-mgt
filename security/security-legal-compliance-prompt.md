# Security and Legal Compliance – Due Diligence

I want us to turn our attention to security and legal compliance. This must be approached as a structured compliance exercise, with clearly defined requirements, strict processes, measurable standards and verifiable evidence of compliance.

Our potential markets include the **US, Canada, UK and EU**. Each has legal, regulatory and industry standards that may apply to MyBizz, depending on our services, operations and the data we process. Examples include GDPR, UK GDPR, ICO requirements, Cyber Essentials, California privacy legislation (CCPA/CPRA), and others. Please identify all relevant legislation, regulations and recognised security standards that we need to consider.

This is a serious due diligence exercise. We need to identify obligations arising from potential data breaches, security failures, non-compliance and other threats that could expose MyBizz or its clients to financial loss, regulatory penalties or legal action. Be thorough and diligent; do not treat this as a cursory overview. Distinguish between legally mandatory requirements and voluntary or commercially recommended standards.

The work must produce three documents or clearly defined deliverables:

1. **Legal and Regulatory Compliance Register:** A descriptive document identifying all potentially applicable legislation, regulations and security standards across our target markets, explaining their requirements and how they apply specifically to MyBizz.

2. **Consolidated Compliance Requirements:** A single, deduplicated list of all applicable requirements, consolidating common obligations across the various standards. Where requirements overlap, adopt the most stringent applicable requirement as our baseline.

3. **Technical Compliance Specification:** A practical technical specification explaining precisely how MyBizz must satisfy these consolidated requirements. This must identify the necessary technical controls, development practices, tools and skills, measurable compliance metrics, required documentation and evidence, and the audit criteria against which our application and development processes will be assessed.

The ultimate objective is to establish a defensible, auditable compliance framework that guides development and provides demonstrable evidence that MyBizz meets its legal and security obligations.
