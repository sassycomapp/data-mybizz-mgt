## 2. The Two-Step Product Ecosystem

### Step 1: Front Desk Assistant (MVP — January 2027)
A lightweight, focused lead-response and booking assistant for independent consultants and small service businesses (1–10 people).
- Connects to Gmail/Microsoft 365, WhatsApp (via Evolution API QR pairing), SMS (via SMSGate Android gateway), website forms, and Google Calendar.
- Captures inbound enquiries, drafts replies in the owner's voice (defaulting to **Approve-Before-Send**), books meetings, and runs 1-to-1 follow-ups.
- Uses **OpenRouter BYOK** so clients control their own AI spend ceiling, and provisions a dedicated **Cloudflare R2** bucket per workspace for automated backups and webhook archival.
- **Strict Boundaries:** Never used for bulk SMS/WhatsApp marketing blasts or cold outreach, and never handles end-customer payments or financial information.

### Step 2: Mybizz CS (Full Operating System — 1 April 2027)
Not a booking app, a CRM, a website builder, or an invoicing tool—it is the whole operating system of a consulting or service business. Each client receives their own isolated application instance on Hetzner Cloud, on their own domain, with their own database, including:
- Public website (with 4 homepage variants), blog, and landing pages
- Booking and scheduling for every service delivery model (in-person, mobile/on-site, live online, deliverables, group sessions)
- Payment processing through the client's own Stripe and PayPal accounts
- CRM, contact management, sales pipeline, companies, and online quotes
- Team email on the client's own domain (via Amazon SES) and email marketing
- Invoicing, built-in accounts (with Wave/Xero/QuickBooks exports), and time tracking
- Customer portal, reviews/testimonials, AI helper, and **WhatsUp** spoken voice assistant
- Daily immutable backups and archival history in the client's own Cloudflare R2 bucket

## 3. The Problem Mybizz Solves

A small consulting or service business trying to operate professionally online buys and wires together five to seven separate tools: a website and hosting, a booking system, a payment gateway, a CRM, an email marketing platform, and an invoicing tool.

The problem has five compounding parts:
1. **Money.** Several subscriptions and setup costs (typically £240+/month for a small team), increasing with every team member.
2. **Integration.** Tools do not share customer, booking, payment, or invoice data, forcing the owner to re-type data manually.
3. **Technical burden.** The owner becomes a part-time IT administrator instead of serving clients.
4. **Data.** Client records are scattered across disconnected systems.
5. **Ownership.** On marketplace platforms (Booksy, Vagaro, Mindbody), the business competes beside rivals on its own listing, pays up to 20–23.5% commissions, and loses its customer relationships if it leaves.

Mybizz answers all five with simple, isolated, client-owned software at one predictable monthly price.

## 5. Brand Story & Founder Credibility

Mybizz was built from lived operational experience—not from a whiteboard.

Before building software, the founder spent years setting up and running life-support operations in some of the world's most remote environments, from the Congo jungle to Antarctica. In remote operations, systems must be **simple, secure, robust, and fit for purpose**—if communication breaks down, operations fail.

When the founder set out to run an online consulting and service business, he hit the exact opposite: a fragmented mess of disconnected tools, hidden fees, and endless technical setup. Unable to find one clean, owned platform that solved the whole problem, he built it himself.

> *"I built Mybizz because I needed it. I couldn't find one platform that gave a service business everything it needed without a dozen subscriptions, technical headaches, or losing ownership of its customers. So I built one."*

## 6. Brand Personality & Tone of Voice

- **Character:** Clear, trustworthy, calm, practical, human, and polished—like a dependable operations partner.
- **Avoid:** Over-technical jargon ("AI orchestration", "LLM agents"), flashy gradients, playful gimmicks, exaggerated guarantees, or pushy sales hype.
- **Primary Brand Message:** **One platform. One price. You own it.**
- **Front Desk Assistant Message:** **Never miss a qualified enquiry again. Your AI front desk responds, books calls, and follows up—using your inbox, WhatsApp, SMS, calendar, and rules.**

## 8. Hosting, Security & Ownership Promises

- **EU Hosting on Hetzner Cloud:** Client instances, primary/hot-standby PostgreSQL databases, Redis queues, and Evolution API WhatsApp containers run on **Hetzner Cloud** inside the EU (Germany/Finland, fully UK GDPR adequate).
- **Per-Client Cloudflare R2 Storage:** Every workspace provisions its own isolated **Cloudflare R2 bucket** for automated backups, raw webhook archival, and full data portability.
- **Complete Ownership & No Lock-In:** Month-to-month billing with no contracts; clients export all data as CSV or take their full R2 backup archive at any time.
- **Merchant of Record Billing:** **Paddle** handles global checkout, VAT/sales tax collection, and compliance.
