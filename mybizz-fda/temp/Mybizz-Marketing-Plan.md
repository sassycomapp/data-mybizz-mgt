## 1. The Bootstrap Reality & Capital Path (Zero-Loan Model)

With **no external loans and no debt repayments**, the entire financial picture shifts dramatically in your favour:

1. **Hetzner Cloud Replaces DigitalOcean:** Running PostgreSQL primary-standby replication (~£20/month) and isolated client Anvil containers (~£2.00/client/month) on **Hetzner Cloud (EU/EEA)** cuts your base monthly overhead from **£427/month down to ~£252/month** (before marketing) and boosts your per-client net contribution on Mybizz CS to **~£98.45/month**.
2. **Lower Seed Capital Requirement (~£1,350 instead of £3,000):** Without loan interest or expensive managed databases, you only need **~£1,350 in interim revenue** to incorporate **Mybizz-Live Ltd** in the UK, cover initial statutory/compliance fees, and fund your first 3 months of Hetzner infrastructure until operational break-even.
3. **Accessible Interim Offer (£45/mo or £120 3-Month Founding Pass):** Instead of an expensive £500 upfront fee, **Front Desk Assistant** is priced at **£45/month ($59/mo)** or a **£120 upfront 3-Month Founding Pass** (setup included). Just **11 Founding Passes (£1,320)** fund your entire launch with zero debt.

## 3. Why the Staged Marketing Budget Works

* **Why £0/month in Stage 1:** When you only need 11–15 early clients, cold ads waste money. Direct, personalised outreach costs nothing and converts at 10–20%.
* **Why £350/month in Stage 2 is NOT for cold ads:** On LinkedIn and Google Search, B2B clicks cost £3–£10 each; £350/month is too small to feed ad algorithms. Instead, use £350/month for content repurposing, SEO articles, and seeding micro-creators.
* **Why $1,000/month (~£790/month) unlocks at 15–20 clients:** Once 15+ Mybizz CS clients are paying £119/month (~£1,476/month net contribution), the business comfortably self-funds a **$1,000/month (£790/month)** paid search and retargeting budget while remaining profitable every single month.

## 5. Stage 1 Sniper & Guerrilla Execution Playbook (£0 Budget)

### Tactic 1: The 50-Prospect Sniper List
Build a focused spreadsheet of **50 UK, US, or South African independent consultants, coaches, therapists, or small agencies** (1–10 staff) who:
1. Have an active website with a basic contact form or email link (no instant booking/WhatsApp response).
2. Sell services worth £200+ per client (so missing even one enquiry costs them more than a whole year of Front Desk Assistant).

### Tactic 2: 60-Second Personalised "Teardown" Videos
Record 5 to 10 personalised screen-capture videos per day (using free OBS or Loom) showing the prospect's actual website:
* **0–10 sec (Hook):** *"Hi [Name], I tested how a new client enquires on [Company Website]—and spotted a quick fix that could save you 2–3 lost leads a month."*
* **10–40 sec (Show, Don't Tell):** Show their contact form, then show a live 15-second demo of **Front Desk Assistant** replying to a WhatsApp/Email enquiry in their tone of voice and booking directly into Google Calendar.
* **40–60 sec (Low-Friction Offer):** *"I'm onboarding 11 Founding Pilot businesses this month before our full UK platform launch. I'll set this up for you personally for £120 for 3 full months—no contract, and you lock in Founding Member status. Want me to send over the 2-minute overview?"*

### Tactic 3: Daily "Build-in-Public" & Guerrilla Content
Turn your daily sniper outreach into social proof without spending extra hours creating content from scratch:
* **Daily LinkedIn Post (Personal Profile):** Share honest updates (*"From running life-support camps in Antarctica to bootstrapping a UK software company with £0: here is what I learned pitching 10 consultants today..."*).
* **Guerrilla Campaign — "RIP to the 12-Tool Stack":** Publish side-by-side comparisons and short 30–45 second videos exposing how small service businesses waste £240+/month on disconnected subscriptions (website + Calendly + Mailchimp + HubSpot + FreshBooks + AI tools) when one integrated platform does it all.
* **YouTube Shorts / Reels Repurposing:** Cut your best teardown examples and founder lessons into 30–45 second vertical clips driving viewers to your LinkedIn profile and demo link.

## 8. Non-Negotiable Channel & Compliance Guardrails

1. **Zero Bulk Blasts (Permanent Rule):** Front Desk Assistant and Mybizz CS must **never** be used for bulk SMS/WhatsApp marketing blasts or cold spam campaigns. All WhatsApp (Evolution API) and SMS (SMSGate) messaging is strictly 1-to-1 inbound response, booking confirmation, and 1-to-1 follow-up, with instant automated `STOP` opt-out handling.
2. **Outreach Safety:** Keep personal LinkedIn/DM outreach to 20–30 tailored messages per day per platform, and follow UK PECR / GDPR rules (corporate email outreach must clearly identify the sender and include a simple opt-out; personal/sole-trader WhatsApp outreach requires prior consent or an existing relationship).
3. **No Financial Data in MVP:** Front Desk Assistant never processes end-customer payments or stores card/banking details.
