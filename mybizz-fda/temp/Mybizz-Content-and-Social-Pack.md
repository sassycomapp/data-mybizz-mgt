> Last week I audited 20 independent consulting websites in the UK.
>
> Here is what happened when I tried to enquire as a potential client:
> - 14 had a basic "Contact Us" form that vanished into an inbox
> - 4 listed a personal mobile number with no automated response
> - Only 2 let me book a discovery call immediately
>
> When a prospective client reaches out at 7:30 PM, they are usually messaging 2 or 3 consultants at once. The first person to reply helpfully and offer a calendar slot wins the client.
>
> That's why I built **Front Desk Assistant**. It watches your Email, WhatsApp, and SMS, drafts a reply using your actual services and tone of voice, and offers your live Google Calendar slots—while keeping you in full control with one-click approval.
>
> Simple. No bloated CRM. Just zero missed enquiries.

### Post 3: Why "Approve-Before-Send" Matters for Consultants
> Most consultants refuse to touch AI messaging tools for one good reason:
>
> They are terrified the AI will hallucinate a price, promise a deadline they can't keep, or sound like a cheesy robot to a high-value client.
>
> I wouldn't let an unreviewed bot email my clients either.
>
> That's why **Front Desk Assistant** defaults to **Approve-Before-Send**:
> 1. An enquiry arrives via Email, WhatsApp, or SMS.
> 2. The assistant extracts the lead's details, checks your calendar, and drafts a tailored reply in your voice.
> 3. You review and approve it in one click—or let it auto-acknowledge basic enquiries while locking pricing and proposals behind your approval.
>
> Technology should protect your reputation, not gamble with it.
