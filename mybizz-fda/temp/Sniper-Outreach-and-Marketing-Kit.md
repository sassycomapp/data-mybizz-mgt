# Sniper Outreach & Marketing Kit — Front Desk Assistant

**Launch Window:** January 2027 (Pre-Incorporation Bootstrap Phase)  
**Offers:** £120 Upfront (3-Month Founding Pass, Setup Included) | £45/Month Standard Plan  
**Target Budget:** £0/Month (100% Organic Sniper & Guerrilla Execution)  
**Target Markets:** United Kingdom (Primary), United States, Canada, EU/EEA, Switzerland

---

## 1. Objective & Daily Rule

Your sole goal in Phase 1 is to convert **12 to 15 Founding Users** into paying customers (**£1,440–£1,800 cash collected**) so you can register **Mybizz-Live Ltd** in the UK and fund your initial Hetzner Cloud runway with **zero debt**.

- **Time Allocation:** Once the Front Desk Assistant MVP is live, dedicate **2 focused hours per day** (Monday to Friday) to sniper prospecting and outreach.
- **Compliance Guardrail:** Never send cold bulk blasts on WhatsApp or SMS. Use LinkedIn or corporate B2B email for initial outreach, and only message prospects on WhatsApp or SMS after they explicitly reply or opt in.

---

## 2. Where to Find Your First 50 Prospects (Zero Cost)

Focus on independent consultants, coaches, bookkeepers, and small service agencies (1–5 people) who already invest in a website (proving they have budget) but clearly lose leads after hours.

1. **LinkedIn Search (Free Account):**
   - Search `"Founder" OR "Director" OR "Principal" OR "Consultant"` + location `United Kingdom` + niche keywords (`Bookkeeping`, `HR Consultant`, `Executive Coach`, `Marketing Agency`, `Physiotherapist`, `Tax Advisor`).
   - Filter for active profiles (posted in the last 30 days) that include a website link.
2. **Google Maps & Niche Directories:**
   - Search `"independent business consultant UK"`, `"boutique accounting firm London"`, `"executive coach Manchester"`, or `"private physiotherapy clinic UK"`.
   - Open 15–20 websites at a time and run the **3-Minute Teardown Scorecard** below.
3. **Your Personal & Warm Network:**
   - List 15–20 former colleagues, contractors, or small-business owners from your remote operations career who either run a service business or can introduce you to two people who do.

---

## 3. The 50-Prospect Sniper Research Scorecard

Score every prospect from **1 to 5** inside `Front-Desk-Sniper-Tracker.xlsx` before recording a video. Prioritise prospects scoring **4 or 5**.

| Criterion | What to Look For on Their Website | Points | Why It Makes Them a Buyer |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Slow Enquiry Form** | Only a static `Contact Us` email form (no instant booking or fast chat). | +1 | Prospects go cold waiting 12–24 hours for a manual reply. |
| **2. Mobile / WhatsApp Gap** | Displays a mobile number on their site, meaning they manually reply between client calls. | +1 | Front Desk Assistant automates WhatsApp & SMS replies in their own voice. |
| **3. Calendar Friction** | Asks visitors to *"email us with your availability"* instead of offering live Google Calendar slots. | +1 | Eliminates multi-email scheduling ping-pong. |
| **4. Solo / Small Team (1–5)** | Owner-operator or tiny team where the founder personally handles admin. | +1 | They feel the lead-response pain personally every day. |
| **5. Active Online Presence** | Posts on LinkedIn or runs Google/social traffic to their site. | +1 | They are actively paying or working for traffic, making missed enquiries painful. |

## 4. The 60-Second Personalised Teardown Video Script

Use **Loom (free tier)** or **OBS Studio (free)** with your webcam bubble in the corner and their actual website open on your screen. Keep the video between **60 and 75 seconds**.

### 0:00–0:12 — The Personal Hook (Pattern Interrupt)
> *"Hi [First Name], David here—I’m not pitching a marketing agency or trying to sell you ads. I just spent two minutes looking at [Company Name]’s website and spotted one small gap in your enquiry flow that might be costing you booked calls."*

### 0:12–0:35 — The Specific Observation (Show Their Screen)
> *"Right now, if a prospective client lands on your site at 7 PM and fills out your contact form—or texts your mobile—they have to wait until you’re out of meetings to get a reply and go back and forth on calendar times. In consulting and services, the first person to reply within five minutes usually wins the client."*

### 0:35–0:55 — The Solution & Founder Credibility
> *"I used to run remote life-support operations in places like the Congo and Antarctica where missing a message wasn't an option, so I built a lightweight Front Desk Assistant for small consultancies. It connects to your Gmail, WhatsApp, SMS, and Google Calendar—replies in your exact voice, answers FAQs, and books calls automatically. Nothing ever sends without your approval unless you switch auto-send on."*

### 0:55–1:10 — The Low-Risk Founding Pass Offer
> *"I’m onboarding my first 15 UK Founding Users this month for £120 upfront for a full 3 months—and I’ll configure the whole thing with you in 20 minutes. Want me to send over a live test number so you can try texting it yourself?"*

>
> *No hard sell—let me know what you think!"*

### E. Gentle 4-Day Follow-Up (Send Once If No Reply)
> *"Hi [First Name], just floating this back up in case it got buried during a busy week. Did you get a chance to glance at that 60-second video for [Company Name]? Happy to send over a live test link if you’re curious—otherwise no pressure at all!"*

## 6. Objection-Handling Cheat Sheet

| Prospect Objection | Calm, Empathetic Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"I don't trust AI to talk to my clients."** | *"I completely agree—that’s why new accounts default to **Approve-Before-Send** mode. The assistant drafts the reply and suggests calendar slots, and you approve it with one tap on your phone or computer before anything goes out."* |
| **"I already use Calendly / Acuity."** | *"Calendly is great once someone clicks the link, but most prospects email or text with questions first. Front Desk Assistant answers their question in your voice, qualifies them, and hands them your booking link automatically."* |
| **"I'm too busy to set up another tool."** | *"That’s why the £120 Founding Pass includes **Done-With-You setup**. We jump on a 20-minute call, connect your Gmail, WhatsApp, and Google Calendar, and test it live together."* |
| **"How much does the AI cost on top?"** | *"You plug in your own OpenRouter key with a hard monthly spend ceiling (usually £2–£5/month for a small consultancy), so you pay raw wholesale model rates with zero markup from us."* |

## 7. Daily 2-Hour January Execution Routine

Repeat this exact loop Monday to Friday:

1. **00:00–00:30 (30 mins) — Research & Score:** Find **5 new prospects**, score them in `Front-Desk-Sniper-Tracker.xlsx`, and note one specific website observation for each.
2. **00:30–01:15 (45 mins) — Record Teardowns:** Record **5 personalised 60-second Loom/OBS videos** using the script in Section 4.
3. **01:15–01:45 (30 mins) — Send & Follow Up:** Send today's **5 LinkedIn DMs / emails** and send your 4-day follow-up to any silent prospects from earlier in the week.
4. **01:45–02:00 (15 mins) — Build in Public:** Post **1 short update** on LinkedIn from `Mybizz-Content-and-Social-Pack.md` sharing what you observed or built today.
