# Mybizz FDA — Marketing Plan

**Product:** Mybizz FDA (Mybizz Front Desk Assistant), mybizz.live
**Price:** £45 a month, whole team included, month to month
**Marketing budget:** £0 a month; direct one-to-one outreach and building in public
**Corporate colour:** Professional Blue

---

## 1. The Aim

- Win about **5 paying clients** first, then grow steadily by personal, one-to-one outreach.
- The funding targets that Mybizz FDA's sales serve are in `business/mybizz-live-business-plan.md`.

---

## 2. Three Stages

| Stage | Goal | What is sold | Channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. First clients** | 5 paying clients: costs covered | Mybizz FDA at £45 a month | 50-prospect sniper list, 60-second personal teardown videos, daily build-in-public posts on LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts |
| **2. Mybizz-Live Ltd** | Register Mybizz-Live Ltd from income; switch WhatsApp on for every client | Mybizz FDA at £45 a month | The same, plus client stories and referrals from happy clients |
| **3. Steady growth** | About 40 clients | Mybizz FDA at £45 a month | The same, plus short case-study videos and UK business communities |

**Why £0:** at £45 a month, paid clicks cost more than a client is worth in the first months. Personal outreach costs only time and converts far better.

---

## 3. Positioning

- **Primary message:** Never miss a qualified enquiry again.
- **What it does, in one line:** Your AI front desk replies in your voice, books the call and follows up, on email, your website and SMS, and nothing goes out without your approval until you say so.
- **Founder hook:** a project manager who ran life-support operations at remote sites, from the Congo jungle to Antarctica, where a missed message meant failure.
- **Proof anyone can test:** send an enquiry through mybizz.live; Mybizz's own front desk is Mybizz FDA.
- **Tone:** direct, honest, calm and practical. Relatable beats impressive.

**Channel claims, at launch:** email (by forwarding), website form, website chat with voice, and SMS from the client's own phone. WhatsApp is announced only when it is switched on.

## 4. Stage 1 Playbook (£0)

### Tactic 1: The 50-prospect sniper list
Build a list of 50 independent consultants, coaches, therapists and small agencies (one to ten people) in the United Kingdom first, then the United States, Canada and Europe, who:
1. Have a website with only a contact form, an email address or a phone number (no instant reply, no booking).
2. Sell work worth £200 or more per client, so one missed enquiry costs more than a year of Mybizz FDA.

Track them in `fda-sniper-tracker.xlsx`.

### Tactic 2: 60-second personal teardown videos
Record five to ten short screen videos a day on the prospect's own website (`fda-sniper-outreach-kit.md`, section 4).

### Tactic 3: Daily build-in-public
One honest post a day on LinkedIn, cut into short videos for YouTube Shorts (`fda-content-and-social-pack.md`).

## 7. Rules That Never Bend

1. **No bulk messages:** Mybizz's own outreach is personal and one to one, exactly as Mybizz FDA's rules require of clients.
2. **Outreach within the law:** 20–30 tailored messages a day per platform; business emails name the sender and offer a simple way to opt out (UK PECR and GDPR); WhatsApp and SMS only to people who already know the founder or who replied first.
3. **Honest claims:** only claim channels and features that are live today.
