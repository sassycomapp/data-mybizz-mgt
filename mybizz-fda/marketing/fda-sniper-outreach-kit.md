# Mybizz FDA — Sniper Outreach Kit

**Offer:** Mybizz FDA, £45 a month, whole team included, month to month
**Budget:** £0 a month
**Markets:** United Kingdom first, then the United States, Canada, Europe and Switzerland

---

## 1. Goal and Daily Rule

- **Goal:** 5 paying clients first (Mybizz FDA's fixed costs covered), then about 2 new clients a week.
- **Time:** 2 focused hours a day, Monday to Friday, once Mybizz FDA is live.
- **Rule:** never send bulk messages. Start on LinkedIn or business email; use WhatsApp or SMS only with people who replied first or already know you.

---

## 2. Where to Find the First 50 Prospects

1. **LinkedIn (free account):** search "Founder" OR "Director" OR "Principal" OR "Consultant", location United Kingdom, plus a niche (Bookkeeping, HR Consultant, Executive Coach, Marketing Agency, Physiotherapist, Tax Adviser). Keep people who posted in the last 30 days and list a website.
2. **Google Maps and directories:** search phrases such as "independent business consultant UK", "boutique accounting firm London", "executive coach Manchester". Open 15–20 websites at a time and score them (section 3).
3. **Your own network:** list 15–20 former colleagues and contacts who run a service business or can introduce you to two who do.

---

## 3. The Prospect Scorecard

Score each prospect from 0 to 5 in `fda-sniper-tracker.xlsx`. Contact prospects scoring 4 or 5 first.

| Criterion | What to look for | Points | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slow enquiry form | Only a "Contact us" form or email address | +1 | Enquirers wait hours for a reply |
| Phone gap | A mobile number on the site, answered by hand | +1 | Mybizz FDA answers texts in the owner's voice |
| Calendar friction | "Email us your availability" instead of real times | +1 | Mybizz FDA offers real free times and books them |
| Small team (1–5) | The owner handles the admin | +1 | The owner feels every missed enquiry |
| Active online | Posts on LinkedIn or advertises | +1 | Paying for traffic makes missed enquiries painful |

## 4. The 60-Second Teardown Video

Use Loom (free) or OBS Studio (free), with your camera in the corner and the prospect's website on screen. Keep it to 60–75 seconds.

**0:00–0:12 — Personal hook**
> "Hi [First Name], David here. I'm not selling ads or a marketing agency. I spent two minutes on [Company Name]'s website and spotted one small gap in your enquiry flow that could be costing you booked calls."

**0:12–0:35 — What you saw**
> "Right now, if someone fills in your form at 7 pm or texts your mobile, they wait until you're out of meetings for a reply, then go back and forth about times. In consulting and services, the first person to reply helpfully usually wins the work."

**0:35–0:55 — The answer and your story**
> "I used to run remote life-support operations, in places like the Congo and Antarctica, where missing a message wasn't an option. So I built Mybizz FDA, a simple front desk for small firms. It replies to your emails, website enquiries and texts in your own voice, answers your FAQs and offers real times from your Google Calendar. Nothing goes out without your one-click approval unless you switch that on."

**0:55–1:10 — The offer**
> "It's £45 a month for your whole team, month to month, no contract. Try it now: send an enquiry on mybizz.live and see what comes back. Shall I send you the two-minute overview?"

## 5. Outreach Scripts

### A. LinkedIn connection request (under 250 characters, no pitch)
> "Hi [First Name], I came across [Company Name] while researching independent [niche] in the UK. Enjoyed your recent post on [topic], and I'd like to connect."

### B. LinkedIn message (24 hours after connecting, with the video)
> "Thanks for connecting, [First Name]. I looked at [Company Name]'s enquiry flow and recorded a 60-second video showing how you could answer and book after-hours enquiries by email, website and text, without changing your website: [Loom link]. No hard sell. I'd value your view."

### C. Business email (sender named, simple opt-out)
**Subject:** A quick observation on [Company Name]'s enquiry flow
> "Hi [First Name],
>
> I spent two minutes on [Company Name]'s website today and recorded a 60-second walkthrough showing how your form and phone enquiries could get a reply in minutes and a booked call in your Google Calendar, without hiring an assistant or changing your website.
>
> Here is the video: [Loom link]
>
> Mybizz FDA is £45 a month for your whole team, month to month. You can test it by sending an enquiry on mybizz.live.
>
> Best regards,
> David [Surname] | Mybizz (mybizz.live)
>
> P.S. If you'd rather not hear from me again, reply "no thanks" and I won't."

### D. Warm network message (people who know you)
> "Hi [First Name], hope you're well! After years running remote operations, I've built Mybizz FDA, a simple AI front desk for consultants and small service businesses. It replies to enquiries by email, website chat and text in the owner's own voice and books calls straight into their Google Calendar. Do you know one or two people who hate missing leads while they're busy with clients? They can try it on mybizz.live."

### E. One gentle follow-up (4 days later, once only)
> "Hi [First Name], just floating this back up in case it got buried in a busy week. Did you get a chance to watch the 60-second video for [Company Name]? If you're curious, you can try it on mybizz.live. Otherwise, no pressure at all."

## 7. Daily 2-Hour Routine

Monday to Friday:

1. **0:00–0:30 — Research and score:** find 5 new prospects, score them in `fda-sniper-tracker.xlsx` and note one observation about each website.
2. **0:30–1:15 — Record:** record 5 teardown videos (section 4).
3. **1:15–1:45 — Send and follow up:** send today's 5 messages and the one-time follow-up to anyone silent for 4 days.
4. **1:45–2:00 — Build in public:** post one short update from `fda-content-and-social-pack.md`.
