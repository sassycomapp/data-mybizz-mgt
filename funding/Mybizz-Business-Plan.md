### The Founder

Mybizz was built from lived operational experience, not a whiteboard. Before building software, the founder spent years setting up and running remote life-support operations in demanding environments—from the Congo jungle to Antarctica—where systems had to be simple, self-contained, resilient, and fit for purpose.

## 3. The Problem

A small consulting or service business trying to operate professionally online typically wires together five to seven separate subscriptions: a website builder, an online booking tool, a payment gateway, a CRM, an email marketing platform, an invoicing package, and an AI writing tool.

This creates five compounding problems:

1. **Money:** Five to seven separate monthly subscriptions (typically **£240+/month** for a small team) before transaction fees or per-seat add-ons.
2. **Fragmentation:** Bookings do not automatically create CRM records; payments do not automatically generate invoices; enquiries slip through the cracks between email, WhatsApp, and SMS.
3. **Technical Burden:** The business owner spends hours configuring DNS, SSL, webhooks, and Zapier bridges instead of serving clients.
4. **Data Silos:** Customer records live across four disconnected tools with no single source of truth.
5. **Marketplace Lock-In:** Discovery marketplaces (such as Booksy, Vagaro, or Mindbody) place competitors directly beside the business's profile, charge up to **20–40%** in commissions or boost fees, and retain ownership of the customer relationship.

**Mybizz solves all five at once:** one setup, one connected platform, one predictable price, and 100% client data ownership.

## 4. The Products

### 4.1 Interim MVP: Front Desk Assistant (Launching January 2027)

**Front Desk Assistant** is the lightweight, fast-to-deploy MVP of Mybizz CS, designed specifically for independent consultants and small agencies (1–10 people):

- **What It Does:** Captures inbound leads from Gmail/Microsoft 365, website forms, WhatsApp (via Evolution API), and SMS (via SMSGate); classifies enquiries; drafts replies in the owner's voice (via OpenRouter BYOK); proposes available Google Calendar slots; and runs 1-to-1 no-response follow-ups.
- **Safety & Compliance Guardrails:** Defaults to **approve-before-send** mode; strictly prohibits bulk SMS/WhatsApp marketing blasts or cold outreach; never processes payments or end-customer financial data.
- **Pricing:** **£45/month** standard subscription, or a **£120 upfront 3-month Founding Pass** (includes Done-With-You onboarding).
- **Commercial Role:** Generates immediate bootstrap cash flow from January 2027 to fund Mybizz-Live Ltd incorporation and server infrastructure, while creating a warm upgrade pipeline into Mybizz CS.

### 4.2 Flagship Platform: Mybizz CS (Launching 1 April 2027)

**Mybizz CS** is a complete, private online business operating system for consulting and service businesses:

- **One Platform, One Price:** **£119/month** (or **£1,190/year**, giving 12 months for the price of 10), including **unlimited staff**, zero booking commissions, zero add-ons, and zero usage markups.
- **Complete Feature Coverage:** Public website (4 homepage layouts, on-page editing), online booking (at premises, at customer location with travel time, video/phone calls, group sessions, session packages), Stripe & PayPal integration, CRM & sales pipeline, quotes, invoicing, time tracking, built-in accounts (with Wave/Xero/QuickBooks exports), unlimited domain email & Team Inbox (via Amazon SES), email marketing, blog with social sharing, ClauseBuilder legal pages, customer portal, AI assistance, and **WhatsUp** browser-based voice assistant.
- **Assisted Setup:** Optional one-off Done-For-You configuration at **£119**.
- **True Data Ownership:** Each client runs in an isolated application container with its own database on its own domain. Automated daily/weekly backups, historical logs, and webhook archives are stored in the **client's own Cloudflare R2 bucket**. Clients can export all data and leave at any time with zero lock-in.

## 8. Operations & Technical Architecture (Hetzner + Cloudflare R2)

- **Cloud Hosting (Hetzner Cloud, EU/EEA):** All production infrastructure runs on **Hetzner Cloud** (Germany/Finland data centers, fully UK GDPR adequate) using Docker Compose behind **Caddy** for automated TLS/SSL:
  - **Application Nodes:** Self-hosted **Anvil App Server** (`anvil-app-server`) running isolated client containers (`master_template` + `blank_client_template`), **Evolution API** (multi-tenant WhatsApp WebSocket gateway), and **Redis** (job queues and burst-message debouncing).
  - **Database Cluster:** Primary PostgreSQL server paired with a **live hot-standby replica** (`CX33` nodes from go-live, scaling to `CX43` at 60+ clients) with automated WAL archiving.
- **Per-Client Storage & Backups (Cloudflare R2):** Every client receives their own isolated **Cloudflare R2 bucket** provisioned during onboarding. Daily/weekly database backups, raw webhook payloads, email archives (>90 days), and audit history (>30 days) are stored in the client's own bucket—keeping primary databases fast and guaranteeing instant portability if a client leaves.
- **Centralised Management (`App 5`):** Mybizz's internal management app automates client container provisioning, monitors server memory and daily backup arrival, enforces fair-use limits, tracks per-client AI/email usage, and triggers founder alerts if a client shows inactivity.
- **Security & Compliance:** Hardware security keys on all infrastructure accounts, encrypted credential Vault with TOTP step-up verification, UK ICO registration, Cyber Essentials certification, and UK GDPR / DPA compliance.

## 10. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Slow initial Front Desk Assistant uptake in Jan–Feb 2027** | Fixed monthly overheads prior to full launch are under **£300/mo (incl. VAT)**. Even 3 Founding Passes (£360) in January cover immediate running costs while sniper outreach scales. |
| **Non-recoverable 20% VAT eroding early margins** | Modeled explicitly at +20% across all supplier and marketing lines. Voluntary UK VAT registration immediately after incorporating Mybizz-Live Ltd eliminates UK VAT drag and enables quarterly input VAT reclaims. |
| **Server or database outage** | Hetzner primary + live hot-standby PostgreSQL replication ensures failover in minutes, backed by immutable daily snapshots and WAL archives in each client's Cloudflare R2 bucket. |
| **WhatsApp or SMS channel bans** | Bulk blasts and cold outreach via Evolution API / SMSGate are strictly blocked in software; channels operate exclusively for 1-to-1 inbound replies, booking confirmations, and opt-in follow-ups. |
| **Founder bandwidth bottleneck** | Automated self-service onboarding wizards with per-step help videos, central `master_template` releases, automated App 5 health monitoring, and budgeted part-time support starting at 20 Mybizz CS clients. |
