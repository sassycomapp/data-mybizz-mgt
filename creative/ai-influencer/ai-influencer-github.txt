Here are your quick, step-by-step instructions for starting up and shutting down your local AI Influencer app whenever you want to use it.

Startup Instructions (How to run it)
Open your WSL terminal (your Ubuntu or Linux terminal).

Jump straight into your project folder by typing:

Bash
cd ~/ai-influencer/ai-influencer-main
Start the local server by typing:

Bash
npm run dev
Look at the text output in your terminal. It will give you a local link that looks like this:http://localhost:5173/

Open Google Chrome, click or paste that link into your address bar, and your influencer dashboard will load right up!

Shutdown Instructions (How to close it safely)
Go back to your WSL terminal window where the server is running.

Press Ctrl + C on your keyboard. This stops the local Node server and shuts down the background process safely.

You can then close your WSL terminal window and close your browser tab.

That is all there is to it! Whenever you want to use it again in the future, just follow the 5 quick startup steps above.
