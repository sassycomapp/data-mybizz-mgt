# Mybizz-Live — Launch and Banking Guide (Sole Proprietor to Mybizz-Live Ltd)

**Purpose:** A practical reference explaining how to legally sell **Mybizz FDA** globally from South Africa as a sole proprietor, pass **Paddle** verification, handle **Wise** banking, and stay compliant with **SARS** and **SARB** before incorporating **Mybizz-Live Ltd** in the UK.

---

## 1. Trading Status: South African Sole Proprietor

You do **not** need a UK Limited Company (`Mybizz-Live Ltd`) or a South African CIPC `(Pty) Ltd` to launch Mybizz FDA in January 2027.

- **How You Trade:** You trade in your personal capacity as a **Sole Proprietor** under your own legal name or trading name: **`[Your Full Name] trading as Mybizz`**.
- **Contracting Party:** Until `Mybizz-Live Ltd` is incorporated in the UK, your website Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Paddle account must state your own full legal name and South African residential/business address. Never list an unincorporated `Ltd` name on legal contracts or Paddle applications.

## 2. Paddle Setup & Verification as a South African Individual

Paddle fully supports South African individuals selling software globally.

### What Paddle Requires for Approval
1. **Live Website (`mybizz.live` or dedicated product subdomain):** Must clearly describe what Mybizz FDA does, display the price (**£45/month**), and include working footer links to the **Terms of Service**, **Acceptable Use Policy**, **Privacy Policy**, **Data Processing Agreement** and **Cancellation and Refund Policy**.
2. **Identity & Address Verification (KYC):**
   - Clear copy of your valid **South African Smart ID Card** or **South African Passport**.
   - Recent **Proof of Residential Address** in South Africa (utility bill, municipal rates bill, or bank statement dated within the last 3 months).
3. **Payout Details:** Your bank account details for receiving monthly payouts from Paddle (South African bank account via SWIFT/local payout or Wise receiving details).

### How Paddle Handles Global Sales Tax
- Paddle acts as your **Merchant of Record (MoR)**.
- When a consultant in the UK, US, Canada, or EU buys Mybizz FDA, Paddle automatically calculates, collects, and remits UK VAT (20%), EU VAT, or US/Canadian sales tax.
- **You do not register for UK VAT** while trading as a South African sole proprietor.

## 3. Wise Account Rules for South African Residents

You **can** open a **Wise Personal Account** right now as a South African resident **without** having a registered company—with one important South African Reserve Bank (SARB) detail to understand:

### Wise Personal vs. Wise Business in South Africa
- **Wise Personal Account (Available Today):**
  - **Requirements:** South African ID/passport + proof of South African residential address. No registered company required.
  - **What You Get:** Multi-currency balances where you can hold and convert **GBP, USD, EUR, CAD, and ZAR**, plus a Wise debit card (depending on regional card availability) and low-cost international transfers.
  - **Important SARB Catch on Local GBP Account Details:** Due to South African exchange control rules, Wise restricts South African resident personal accounts from keeping permanent local UK/US bank account numbers (`Sort Code / Account Number` or `ACH Routing`) active indefinitely for third-party commercial inflows, or requires inflows to be repatriated/declared properly. However, you **can** use Wise to receive international payouts, convert GBP/USD at the real mid-market exchange rate, and pay foreign suppliers (Hetzner, Cloudflare, OpenRouter) without paying punitive South African bank FX spreads (2.5%–4%).
- **Wise Business Account:**
  - Requires a registered business entity (such as a CIPC `(Pty) Ltd` in South Africa or your future UK `Mybizz-Live Ltd`). Once `Mybizz-Live Ltd` is registered at Companies House in the UK, you can open a full **Wise Business** account with local UK Sort Code/Account Number, US ACH, and EU IBAN details.

### Practical Payout Setup for January
1. **Checkout:** Use **Paddle** for every subscription (£45/month). Paddle collects payment from clients and pays out directly to your bank account or Wise.
2. **No other checkout:** every sale goes through Paddle, so Paddle handles tax and invoices on every sale.

## 4. South African Tax (SARS) & Exchange Control (SARB)

### Income Tax (SARS)
- **Declaration:** All net income received from Paddle is declared as **sole proprietor / freelance business income** on your personal **SARS ITR12** tax return (and provisional tax returns `IRP6` twice a year once you earn above the tax threshold).
- **Deductible Expenses:** Keep every PDF invoice from **Hetzner**, **Anvil**, **Cloudflare**, **OpenRouter**, and your domain registrar. All of these are legitimate business expenses deducted from your gross revenue before calculating taxable profit.
- **South African VAT:** You do **not** register for South African VAT until your total taxable turnover exceeds **R1,000,000** (~£42,000) in any consecutive 12-month period.

### Exchange Control (SARB)
- Receiving foreign currency (GBP/USD) into South Africa from software exports is 100% legal and encouraged.
- When foreign funds arrive in your South African bank account (e.g., FNB, Standard Bank, Nedbank, Capitec, Investec), you simply submit an online **Balance of Payments (BoP)** declaration on your banking app/portal selecting **`Category 109 — Computer and Information Services`**.

## 5. Transitioning Later to Mybizz-Live Ltd (UK)

Once Mybizz FDA sales build the cash reserve set out in `mybizz-live-budget.xlsx` and you are ready to incorporate **Mybizz-Live Ltd** in the UK:

1. **Incorporate in the UK:** Register `Mybizz-Live Ltd` at Companies House (~£50–£100 via an online formation agent with a UK registered office address).
2. **Open Wise Business (UK):** Register Wise Business using your UK Certificate of Incorporation to get full UK GBP, EU EUR, and US USD business banking details.
3. **Create a New Paddle Business Account:** Paddle requires a separate seller account when switching from a Sole Proprietor to a Limited Company. Verify `Mybizz-Live Ltd` on Paddle and point new checkouts to the UK company account.
5. **Re-issue the legal texts:** publish the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Data Processing Agreement and other texts in the name of Mybizz-Live Ltd.
6. **Meta Tech Provider:** verify Mybizz-Live Ltd with Meta as a Tech Provider, then switch WhatsApp on for every Mybizz FDA client from the Mybizz management app.
4. **Cross-Border Tax Check (POEM Rule):** Because you live in South Africa while directing a UK company, consult a South African cross-border tax accountant once revenue scales so you stay compliant with both **HMRC (UK)** and **SARS "Place of Effective Management" (POEM)** rules.
