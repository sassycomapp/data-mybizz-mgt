### Recommended Clause: Reseller & Payment Terms

> **Payments and Reseller of Record**
> 
> Our order process and online payments are conducted and handled by our online reseller and Merchant of Record, **Paddle.com Market Ltd** (and its affiliated entities, "Paddle").
> 
> When you purchase a subscription or digital product from us, you purchase it directly from Paddle. Paddle is responsible for billing, payment processing, issuing valid tax receipts and invoices, collecting applicable VAT/sales taxes, and handling order-related customer service inquiries.
> 
> By completing a purchase, you agree to Paddle’s [Terms of Use](https://paddle.com/legal/terms) and acknowledge their [Privacy Policy](https://paddle.com/legal/privacy).

### Key Points to Include in Surrounding Sections

To ensure your billing terms are completely transparent, make sure to align the rest of your agreement with Paddle’s operational model:

1. **Tax Invoicing & Receipts:** State that official tax invoices will be issued directly by Paddle via email upon completed payment or subscription renewal.

2. **Refunds & Cancellations:** Specify your app's refund policy, noting that approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method by Paddle on your behalf.

3. **Price Changes:** Clarify that subscription renewals will be billed by Paddle at the agreed rate, subject to advance notice for any plan modifications.
