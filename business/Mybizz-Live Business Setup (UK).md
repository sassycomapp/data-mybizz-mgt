# Mybizz-Live Business Setup (UK)

When setting up a **SaaS business in the UK**, selecting the right legal entity and completing the correct registration forms is crucial for managing liability, taxation, and potential fundraising.

Below is an overview of the selected UK legal structure, followed by the specific forms and steps required to register.

### Private Limited Company by Shares (Ltd) — *Recommended for SaaS*

This is the standard structure for software startups. The business has its own separate legal personality, meaning your personal assets are protected (limited liability).

- **Why it fits SaaS:** Allows you to issue shares to founders, employees, or equity investors. IP (software, trademarks, subscription revenue) remains under the company entity.

- **Ownership:** Managed by directors and owned by shareholders. One person can hold both roles.

- ## Forms & Registration Documents Required
  
  To register a **Private Limited Company (Ltd)**, you file primarily through **Companies House** (the UK's registrar of companies).
  
  | **Form / File**                                      | **Purpose**                                                                           | **Notes**                                                                                                                                                |
  | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | **Form IN01** *(Application to Register)*            | The core statutory form to register a new company.                                    | Filed digitally via Companies House WebFiling or using paper form IN01.                                                                                  |
  | **Memorandum of Association**                        | Legal confirmation that the founding shareholders agree to form the company.          | Generated automatically when registering online via Companies House.                                                                                     |
  | **Articles of Association**                          | The company's rulebook governing director powers, share transfers, and voting rights. | You can adopt the standard UK "Model Articles" or upload customized articles.                                                                            |
  | **PSC Register** *(People with Significant Control)* | Identifies individuals holding >25% of shares or voting rights.                       | Included directly within the digital IN01 submission.                                                                                                    |
  | **SIC Code** *(Standard Industrial Classification)*  | Classifies your business activity.                                                    | For a SaaS app, common codes include **62012** (*Business and domestic software development*) or **62090** (*Other IT and computer service activities*). |
  
  ## Step-by-Step Incorporation Checklist
  
  1. **Company Name & Address:**
     
     - Ensure your company name is available on the Companies House register.
     
     - You need an official UK **Registered Office Address** (virtual office services in the UK are fully accepted if you are operating remotely).
  
  2. **Director & Identity Verification:**
     
     - Appoint at least one director (aged 16+).
     
     - Identity verification via **GOV.UK One Login** is standard practice for company directors.
  
  3. **Share Capital Setup:**
     
     - Allocate initial shares (e.g., 100 ordinary shares at £1.00 each).
  
  4. **Filing Method & Cost:**
     
     - **Online Filing:** Costs £100 via the Companies House WebFiling portal (usually processed within 24 hours).
     
     - **Paper Filing:** Costs £124 by post using paper Form IN01 (takes 8–10 days).
  
  5. **Post-Registration (HMRC & Taxes):**
     
     - **Certificate of Incorporation:** Once approved, you receive this official document containing your UK Company Number.
     
     - **Corporation Tax:** You must register with HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) within 3 months of starting to trade.
     
     - **VAT:** You are required to register for UK Value Added Tax if your taxable UK turnover exceeds **£90,000** in a 12-month rolling window (or if you sell digital SaaS products to UK consumers).



1. Step-by-Step Incorporation Process
- Registering a UK Private Limited Company (Ltd) completely remotely as a non-resident founder is straightforward.
  
  ```
  [1. Select Name & Get Address] ➔ [2. Complete Mandatory ID Check] ➔ [3. Submit IN01 online] ➔ [4. Apply for FinTech Bank Account]
  ```
  
  **1.Select Company Name & Business Activity (SIC Code):**Check availability on Companies House.
  
  Choose a unique company name. Select a Standard Industrial Classification (SIC) code for your SaaS app (e.g., **62012** for *Software development* or **62090** for *Other IT services*).
  
  **2.Secure Required Address Services:**Prerequisite before filing.
  
  Purchase a UK **Registered Office Address** service and a **Director's Service Address** service from a registered UK provider (such as Quality Company Formations, Hoxton Mix, or First Formations).
  
  **3.Complete Mandated Identity Verification:**Required for all Directors & PSCs.
  
  All company directors and Persons with Significant Control (PSCs holding >25% of shares) must verify their identity online via **GOV.UK One Login** or an authorized corporate service provider.
  
  **4.File Form IN01 & Incorporate Online:**Fastest via WebFiling portal or an agent.
  
  Submit your incorporation details, share structure (e.g., 100 ordinary shares at £1 each), and default Articles of Association through the Companies House portal (£100 fee). Processing typically takes 24 hours.
  
  **5.Open Your Business Account & HMRC Registration:**Final operational setup.
  
  Once Companies House issues your **Certificate of Incorporation** and 10-digit **Company Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR)**, use these documents to open your digital business bank account.
  
  ## 2. Virtual Address Requirements
  
  UK law (*Companies Act 2006*) strictly distinguishes between different types of addresses. Since you do not reside in the UK, you must purchase an appropriate address package:
  
  | **Address Type**                   | **Purpose**                                                                                         | **Is a Virtual Office Allowed?**                                                               | **Public Record Visibility**                                 |
  | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
  | **Registered Office Address**      | Official legal address for your company where statutory mail from HMRC and Companies House is sent. | **Yes**, provided it is a physical UK location that accepts mail. **PO Boxes are prohibited**. | **Public** on Companies House register.                      |
  | **Director’s Service Address**     | Official contact address for directors/shareholders.                                                | **Yes**, can be the same virtual address as the Registered Office.                             | **Public** (keeps your non-UK home address private).         |
  | **Director's Residential Address** | Director's actual home address abroad.                                                              | **No**, must be your real home address.                                                        | **Private** (stored on secure internal records, not public). |
  
  *Note: Virtual office address plans generally cost around £35 to £100 per year, which includes scanning and forwarding official government mail.*
